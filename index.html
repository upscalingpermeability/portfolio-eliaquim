About Me

Holds a Bachelor’s Degree in Physical Engineering from Universidade Federal do Oeste do Pará (2017) and an Interdisciplinary Bachelor’s Degree in Science and Technology from Universidade Federal do Oeste do Pará (2014), including an academic exchange program in Mechatronics at Universidad de Talca (2014). Earned both a Master’s degree (2019) and a Ph.D. (2024) in Computational Modeling from Laboratório Nacional de Computação Científica. Worked as a public servant in the role of Technology Analyst at the Ministério da Agricultura e Pecuária from 2024 to 2025.

Currently works as a Junior Technologist at the Agência Espacial Brasileira. Has experience in the field of Geosciences, with an emphasis on Remote Sensing.

Currently, I'm deep-diving into modern full-stack ecosystems, exploring the intersection of robust backend logic and fluid frontend interactions.
